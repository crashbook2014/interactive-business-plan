### 🔴 P0 — Unanswered contract type silently adds a 20,000 SAR notice line

**Found by: wodouh-redteam (complete)**

`termQNext()` has no validation on the contract-type step. The toggle
renders with neither option selected, and Next advances. `termCtype()`
returns `null`, which every downstream reader treats as "indefinite
contract." The Article 76 notice line (`tm_m_notice`) is owed only on an
indefinite contract (`app/index.html:7924-7930`). A fixed-term Saudi reader
who skips one toggle is shown 20,000 SAR (6 months at 10,000 wage) as
*certain* entitlement.

`app/index.html:7558-7560` states the product's own rule: *"a blank answer
is reported as missing rather than guessed."* This is the one place that
rule is not followed.

Reproduced by walking the real UI: start `2020-01-01`, end `2026-01-01`,
wage 10,000, notice 60, given 0, contract type never tapped → `tm_m_notice
20,000` in certain column, `termUnassessed` does not mention it.

The free calculator is not affected — `calcEos()` computes no notice line.

**Claude can fix with a test.** Either require `ctype` before leaving step
2 (one line), or when `termCtype() === null` suppress `tm_m_notice`, move
`tm_m_comp` to the two-month floor, and add both to `termUnassessed()`.
Add a test asserting that for any Saudi reader with `term.ctype === null`,
`tm_m_notice` does not appear as a certain line.

### 🟠 P1 — 44 of 58 answer-page titles are truncated mid-phrase

**Found by: wodouh-growth (complete), wodouh-engineer (partial)**

`tools/make-seo.mjs:195-197`. When the suffix is long (e.g. `art. 6 of
the Implementing Regulations | Wodouh` ≈ 48 chars), `maxTopicLen` hits
its floor of `Math.max(10, …)` = 10. The word-boundary guard `lastSpace >
10` can never fire inside a 10-character window, so the title is cut
mid-word. The worst result:

```
Employer m… — art. 6 of the Implementing Regulations | Wodouh
لا يجوز لصاحب العمل… — المادة 6 من اللائحة التنفيذية | وضوح
```

Growth measured 44 of 58 pages affected (22 English, 22 Arabic). August
reported 2; today it is 44. All 58 English titles remain in statute form
rather than question form.

The truncation exists because the topic phrase is a statute sentence
squeezed into 60 characters. Question-form titles fit with room to spare:

| Now | Proposed |
|---|---|
| `End-of-service — Saudi Labor Law, Article 84 \| Wodouh` | `How much end-of-service pay am I owed? — Article 84 \| Wodouh` |
| `Employer m… — art. 6 of the Implementing Regulations \| Wodouh` | `Can my employer keep my passport? — Saudi law \| Wodouh` |

**Note on urgency:** the sitemap was submitted to Search Console on 30
August (per `docs/geo-benchmark.md`). Google has been crawling 58 pages
with these titles for two weeks.

**Claude can fix the truncation bug.** The `Math.max(10, …)` floor at
`make-seo.mjs:196` is the root cause.

**Question-form titles require a `QUESTION_EN` / `QUESTION_AR` column in
the register** — 29 pairs authored by a human, one per topic, so the
generator can assert every row has one (build fails otherwise). This is
the SEO equivalent of the register's sourcing discipline.

**Before regenerating:** run the GEO baseline in `docs/geo-benchmark.md`
(four engines, 15 questions, ~30 min). The before/after is permanently
lost if regenerated first.
