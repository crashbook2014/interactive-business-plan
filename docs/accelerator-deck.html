The Gap Is Real — And Measurable
What she received vs. what she was owed
5,000 → 15,000
SAR — a 3-year employee, post-birth termination
Article 87 of the Saudi Labor Law grants a full award — no reduction — when an employer ends a contract within 3 months of childbirth. Most workers never learn this. She signed the settlement and left.
Source: Saudi Labor Law Art. 87 · Scenario verified in Wodouh test suite
Three reasons the gap
stays hidden
The contract is written by the employer
Non-compete clauses, deduction terms, and notice requirements are drafted to favor the party that hires. Workers sign without parsing any of it.
Legal counsel costs hundreds of riyals and takes days
Workers facing termination need answers in hours — before they sign a settlement. Most cannot afford to wait or pay.
No Arabic-first tool cites the actual law
Generic AI tools guess at article numbers. Without a verified legal register behind the output, the answer is worse than useless — it's confidently wrong.
