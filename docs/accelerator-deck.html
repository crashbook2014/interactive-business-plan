The Problem
"My employer ended my contract. I don't know
if I'm owed money or how much to ask for."
Every terminated Saudi worker, today
1
Contracts are written against workers
Most Saudi employment contracts are drafted by employers. Workers sign without understanding non-compete clauses, deduction terms, or what triggers a full versus reduced award.
2
Legal advice is expensive and slow
A lawyer's consultation costs hundreds of riyals and takes days. Workers facing termination need answers in hours — before signing anything.
3
No Arabic-first tool exists
Generic AI tools hallucinate Saudi Labor Law articles. No product cites sources, verifies claims against the law, or explains entitlements in plain Arabic.
