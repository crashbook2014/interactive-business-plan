## 2. Product — four independent agent passes

### 🔴 The termination funnel asks for money before showing anything — found three separate ways

**wodouh-experience**, walking the app as a just-terminated user: after ~15
fields including wage, both dates, and leave balance, the paywall screen
states "5 entitlements found" and asks for 349 SAR — **without stating a
single riyal figure**. Direct quote: *"That is exactly the moment a
frightened person decides this is a machine that has taken their data and is
now holding it hostage."*

**wodouh-engineer**, reading the code independently: `buildTermPreview()` at
`app/index.html:8284` — the preview is the reader's own answers read back,
with no computed figure.

**wodouh-redteam**, attacking the calculator independently: the *free*
end-of-service calculator (a different screen, reachable without the
termination flow) has a real bug that would make showing a free figure here
dangerous until fixed — see the next item.

**Why this is one item, not three:** the fix experience/engineer are
describing — show the EOS figure free, before the 349 paywall — depends on
red-team's P0 being fixed first, or the free number shown to exactly the
readers most likely to be affected (recent births, force majeure exits) would
be wrong. **Sequence: fix the calculator, then show the figure.**

### 🔴 P0 — the free calculator omits two legal exceptions, silently understating entitlement (wodouh-redteam)

`app/index.html:6227` (`calcEos`). The free calculator offers two endings —
employer-ended / resigned — and applies the resignation reduction
unconditionally. There is no branch for Article 87 (force majeure; marriage
within 6 months; giving birth within 3 months — full award, no reduction) or
Article 81 (employer breach — full award). Both exceptions are implemented
*correctly* in the paid termination assessment; the free front door, which
far more people reach, doesn't have them.

Reproduced by the agent: a woman 2 months post-birth with 3 years' service at
10,000 SAR sees **5,000 SAR** shown; she is owed **15,000**. At 1.5 years the
tool prints "resigning before two years generally earns no award" with no
onward path telling her an exception might apply.

`test/calc-fuzz.test.js:101` currently asserts the wrong (reduced) figure as
correct — the test needs to change alongside the fix, not just the code.

### 🟠 The live public homepage is selling prices the app will never charge (wodouh-engineer)

`index.html:662-671, 738-740` vs. the real catalogue in
`app/index.html:10343-10394`. The homepage shows **65 SAR** for the letter
(real: 149), a **130 SAR "job-change pack"** that doesn't exist in the app at
all, and **325 SAR** for the case file (real: 349 — 325 is the exact figure
the pricing invariant removed in the August catalogue). It also promises
"unlimited scoring, flags, clause explanations — free," which contradicts

This page carries: *"these are launch prices — reserve before launch and your

`docs/pricing.md` itself still shows the old case-file prices (245/395) in
one section, under its own "containment invariant" heading — contradicting
its own August table further down the same file.

### 🟠 The 199 SAR paywall may be selling what the free view already gives away (wodouh-experience)

### 🟠 All 58 new SEO pages have a structural problem, found by two agents independently

**wodouh-growth**, from a search-intent angle: every English title restates
the statute ("What does Article 84 say?") instead of the question a person
types ("How much end-of-service am I owed?"). Two titles are **literally cut
off mid-sentence** in both languages — this is in the generator, not a
one-off.

**wodouh-engineer** and **wodouh-experience**, reading the same pages
independently, found the identical truncation bug and additionally: all 58
pages' call-to-action links to `/app/`, which the pre-launch curtain
currently blocks — `location.replace()` fires immediately, and the page has
no script to route around it (CSP is `default-src 'none'` by design). The
existing curtain test (`soon.test.js:77`) checks `a[href*="app/index.html"]`,
which never matches the real `/app/` href — so this gap wasn't caught.

**Recommendation from growth, worth taking as-is:** do not connect Search
Console until the titles are fixed — the first indexed impression of these
pages should not be a sentence that stops mid-thought.

### 🟡 Article 53 is styled as law despite being disputed (wodouh-engineer)

`app/index.html:4098, 4318, 5455, 8740` render Article 53 with the `.src-line

### 🟡 `ai_analysis` fails open, unlike every other flag (wodouh-redteam)

`app/index.html:7471` — `flagOn("ai_analysis", true)`. Every other flag
(payments, subscriptions, lawyer_desk) falls to a compiled-off constant if
the flags fetch fails; this one defaults to **on**. Not exploitable today
(`ANALYZE_URL` is empty, so there's nothing to turn on), but it means the
console's off switch is not actually a kill switch once AI is live — a
timeout, an offline reader, or a malformed flags response would turn the
upload surface on while the console displays off. `docs/enable-ai-runbook.md`
already says the real off switch is a deploy, so this is a known
architecture, but the flag's own default contradicts its stated design.

### 🟡 A model can cite an unverified article number, by design, with a visible seam (wodouh-redteam)

Contract-review mode lets the model cite any article it's confident about;
the register's absolute rule ("only if verified") is enforced by an amber
warning block on the unverified ones, not by refusal. The mitigation is real
and functioning — but it's a recorded policy divergence from the register's
own stated rule, and needs a founder decision before AI goes live, not
silently carried forward.

### 🟢 No delete-my-data control in the UI, despite the RPC existing (wodouh-experience + this audit)

`delete_my_account()` exists as a `SECURITY DEFINER` function in the database
(confirmed by Supabase's own security advisor, flagged only because it's
callable by `authenticated` — which is presumably intentional for
self-service). But wodouh-experience found **no button, link, or screen in
the app that calls it.** The capability is built server-side and unexposed
client-side.

### 🟢 One grammar tell in otherwise strong Arabic (wodouh-experience)

"باقي لك 2 أسئلة مجانية" should use the Arabic dual ("سؤالان"). Everything
else the agent read felt genuinely written, not translated — this is the one
exception, and it stands out for exactly that reason.
