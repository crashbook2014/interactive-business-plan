01Who we are

A knowledgeable friend, not a law firm

صديق يفهم في العقود، مو مكتب محاماة

Wodouh reads employment, rental, freelance and loan contracts in Saudi Arabia and tells people, in plain Arabic and English, what binds them, what is unusual, and where each point comes from. It also works out what a worker is owed when a job ends.

Our promise: you should never have to take our word for it. Every figure shows its rule, every flag shows its source, and what we could not assess is said out loud.

Calm authority

Precise, short sentences. Verdicts, not lectures.

Warm

Everyday Saudi Arabic, friendly English. We talk to you, not about you.

On your side

Every flag says what it means for you and what to do next.

Who it is for

  • An employee reading an offer before signing.
  • Someone let go last week, working out what they are owed.
  • A tenant, a freelancer, a borrower with a contract in front of them.
  • HR teams and brokers reading contracts every week (Wodouh for business).

What we never claim

  • We are not a law firm and give no legal representation.
  • No guaranteed outcomes, no invented articles, no invented numbers.
  • No licence, seal or approval we do not hold.

02Logo

The lens-check mark

انظر عن قرب، ثم اطمئن
وضوح
WODOUH

Primary lockup. The mark sits on the leading side: left in English, and still left in Arabic, where the Arabic name leads the text.

How it is built

A magnifying glass whose lens resolves into a check: look closely, then feel sure. Three strokes, rounded caps, no fill.

Clear space and size

Keep clear space equal to the lens radius on every side. Smallest sizes: 24 px on screen, 8 mm in print. Below that, use the app icon tile.

Wodouh brand identity guide03
03Logo misuse

Keep it calm and whole

لا تمدّه، ولا تلوّنه، ولا تزخرفه
✕ Stretched
✕ Off-palette colour
✕ Busy background
✕ Rotated
✕ Shadows, glows, 3D

Never in the Wodouh world

  • Law-firm signals: gavels, columns, briefcases, navy and gold.
  • Cold fintech: sterile white, harsh gradients, sharp corners, geometric Kufi.
  • Alarm: bright red, sirens, ALL-CAPS threats. Risks are explained calmly.
  • Emoji as icons. They render differently on every phone and read as chat.

Always

  • The mark exactly as drawn, from the SVG files, never redrawn.
  • One of the three approved colourways.
  • Rounded strokes, generous space, paper calm.
  • Arabic and English given equal care.
  • When in doubt, show a contract being read: clause, highlight, margin note.

Wodouh brand identity guide07
06bSignature motif

The annotated contract

العقد المُعلَّق عليه: هويتنا في صورة واحدة

Real product alwodouh.com home, Arabic. The hero is a contract being read with you.

  1. Margin clause numbers. 5, 7, 3: real clause numbers sit in the margin, like a printed contract.
  2. Colour-coded highlight + margin note. Red "pause before signing", amber "worth negotiating", green "all clear". The note names the clause and the action, never a threat.
  3. Page counter. «صفحة 2 من 6»: we are in the middle of a real document, not a slogan.
  4. The pen note. ✎ one-line verdict in teal, as if written in the margin by a friend.
  5. The unsigned line. «لم يُوقَّع بعد»: the decision is still the reader's.
  6. Score seal. The 0 to 100 ring, stamped at the foot of the page.
  7. Hand-drawn underline. A single pen stroke under the headline. Used once per screen, never as decoration elsewhere.
  8. Sections are clauses. Every section kicker is numbered like a clause, on a ruled card: البند 1 · وش هو وضوح, "Clause 1 · What Wodouh is".
Wodouh brand identity guide07b
07Iconography

Line icons, never emoji

أيقونات خطية، بدون إيموجي

24 px grid · 1.7 to 2.2 stroke · rounded caps and joins · no fill · Teal Ink on a soft-teal tile with a paper border. The same rules as the tab bar.

✓ Do

One idea per icon. Pair every icon with a word; icons never stand alone as the only label.

✕ Don't

Emoji, filled glyphs, gradients, or justice clichés like gavels and columns.

08Voice

Say it like a friend who knows

قلها مثل صديق يفهم

Arabic first

Everyday Saudi (Gulf) Arabic, warm and clear. English is a full equal, plain and professional, never a word-for-word translation.

Show the source

Every figure names its rule; every flag names its article, or says it is our own reading. Say what we could not assess.

No pressure

Many readers have just lost their income. No fear, no false urgency, no countdowns, no "guaranteed".

House style: no em dashes and no slashes in copy; commas and full stops instead. Western numerals. "Wodouh is not a law firm" wherever legal content appears.
