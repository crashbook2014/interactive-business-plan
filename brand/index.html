Brand personality

A knowledgeable friend, not a law firm

Wodouh reads employment, rental, and freelance contracts and explains them like a trusted friend who happens to know contracts well: calm, warm, and on your side. Never intimidating, never bureaucratic, never cold.

Calm authority

Confident and precise without jargon. Short sentences. Verdicts, not lectures.

“This clause is common — nothing to worry about.”

Warmth

Speaks Saudi-everyday Arabic and friendly English. Addresses you directly, before a decision that matters.

«خذ راحتك، بس انتبه للبند الرابع قبل التوقيع.»

On your side

Typography

Soft naskh · humanist sans

Arabic leads in Noto Naskh Arabic — rounded, human curves, never geometric Kufi. English pairs with Nunito, a humanist sans with rounded terminals. Both faces are self-hosted and embedded in the page, so the identity renders the same everywhere — no external font requests.

Employment contract · عقد عمل

Non-compete clause · بند عدم المنافسة

Blocks you from similar work for 2 years across all of Saudi Arabia. That’s unusually broad — ask to narrow it before signing.

Notice period · مدة الإشعار

60 days is on the long side. 30 days is common — worth asking.

Overtime pay · أجر العمل الإضافي

The contract doesn’t say how overtime is paid. Ask for it in writing.

Salary & benefits · الراتب والبدلات

Clearly stated, paid monthly, with housing and transport allowances. All clear.

30 days paid leave — matches the standard. All clear.
