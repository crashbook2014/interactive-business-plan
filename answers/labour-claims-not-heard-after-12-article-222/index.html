Labour claims not heard after 12 months from the end of the relationship — what does Article 222 say?

Last re-checked 31 July 2026.

Labour claims not heard after 12 months from the end of the relationship, unless the court accepts an excuse or the defendant acknowledges the right

Saudi Labor Law, Article 222
