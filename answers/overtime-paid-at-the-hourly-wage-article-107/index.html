Overtime paid at the hourly wage plus 50% of the basic wage — what does Article 107 say?

Last re-checked 31 July 2026.

Overtime paid at the hourly wage plus 50% of the basic wage. Since the 2025 amendment an employer may offer compensatory paid leave instead of payment, but only with the worker's consent

Saudi Labor Law, Article 107
