Final settlement is due within one week of the relationship ending where the employer — what does Article 88 say?

Last re-checked 31 July 2026.

Final settlement is due within one week of the relationship ending where the employer terminated or the contract expired, and within two weeks where the worker resigned. It covers final wages, unused leave and the end-of-service award

Saudi Labor Law, Article 88
