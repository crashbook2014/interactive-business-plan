Non-compete must be in writing and define duration — what does Article 83 say?

Last re-checked 31 July 2026.

Non-compete must be in writing and define duration, geographic area and type of activity. Two years from the end of the relationship is a maximum, not a norm — a longer period is not made valid by agreement

Saudi Labor Law, Article 83
