GOSI and the end-of-service award are separate entitlements — what are the rules in Saudi Arabia?

Last re-checked 31 July 2026.

GOSI and the end-of-service award are separate entitlements. The award under Articles 84 and 85 is paid by the employer and is not reduced by, offset against, or replaced by GOSI pension contributions

No article number. The register records this claim without one, and Wodouh never adds a number the source does not carry.
