Since the 2021 Labor Reform Initiative a worker may transfer employer through Qiwa without — what are the rules in Saudi Arabia?

Last re-checked 31 July 2026.

Since the 2021 Labor Reform Initiative a worker may transfer employer through Qiwa without the current employer's consent in defined cases, including contract expiry, three consecutive months of unpaid wages, and an expired Iqama
