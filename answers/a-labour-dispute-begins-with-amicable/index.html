A labour dispute begins with amicable settlement — what are the rules in Saudi Arabia?

Last re-checked 31 July 2026.

A labour dispute begins with amicable settlement, filed electronically with MHRSD; roughly a week is allowed for direct negotiation, followed by settlement sessions. If it fails, the case may be referred to the labour court within 21 working days of the first complaint
