Termination without award or notice is confined to the specified cases in Article 80 — what does Article 80 say?

Last re-checked 31 July 2026.

Termination without award or notice is confined to the specified cases in Article 80, treated by the labour courts as an exceptional disciplinary provision rather than a routine route. The employer must let the worker state their case, document the reason, and notify in writing

Saudi Labor Law, Article 80
