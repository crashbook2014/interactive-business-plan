Saudi nationals registered before 3 July 2024 contribute under the legacy system — what are the rules in Saudi Arabia?

Last re-checked 31 July 2026.

Saudi only. Saudi nationals registered before 3 July 2024 contribute under the legacy system: 21.5% total, employer 11.75% and employee 9.75%. Those registered on or after 3 July 2024 fall under the new system, which rose in July 2026 to 23.5% total, employer 12.75% and employee 10.75%
