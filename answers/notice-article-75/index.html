Notice — what does Article 75 say?

Last re-checked 31 July 2026.

Notice, monthly-paid indefinite contract: employer 60 days, employee 30 days. Applies to indefinite contracts only — a non-Saudi's contract is always fixed-term (art. 37), so this rule does not reach them

Saudi Labor Law, Article 75
