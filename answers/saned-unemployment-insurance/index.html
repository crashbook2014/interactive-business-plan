SANED unemployment insurance — what are the rules in Saudi Arabia?

Last re-checked 31 July 2026.

Saudi only. SANED unemployment insurance: available to Saudi nationals who lost work involuntarily, are actively seeking work, have contributed for at least 12 months in the past 36, and are registered with Taqat (HRDF). Pays 60% of the average covered monthly wage over the previous two years for the first three months, capped at SAR 9,000, then 50%, for up to 12 months. Funded 0.75% employer and 0.75% employee
