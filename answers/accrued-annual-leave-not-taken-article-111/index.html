Accrued annual leave not taken is paid on leaving — what does Article 111 say?

Last re-checked 31 July 2026.

Accrued annual leave not taken is paid on leaving, for the whole period in which it was not used, and pro-rata for the part-year worked. Calculated on the last wage. The right cannot be waived by either party

Saudi Labor Law, Article 111
